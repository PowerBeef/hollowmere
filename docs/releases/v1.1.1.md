**Hollowmere v1.1.1**: two small changes to v1.1.

**Download `Hollowmere-v1.1.1.zip`** below. It holds `Hollowmere.gba`: play it in [mGBA](https://mgba.io) or another accurate emulator, or on a GBA with a flash cartridge (it saves to SRAM). Saves from v1.1 and v1.0 work.

- **The portraits on the walls no longer have a black blob over the bottom of their frames.** The sitter's dark coat was painted past the edge of the canvas, in v1.0 as well.
- **The ending credits name the game's director, PowerBeef.**

Everything else is v1.1: see its [release notes](https://github.com/PowerBeef/hollowmere/releases/tag/v1.1) and the [changelog](https://github.com/PowerBeef/hollowmere/blob/v1.1.1/CHANGELOG.md). The known limits are unchanged: a few close views of the hall staircase and the chapel's pews run at 15 fps, and nothing has been run on a console.

Building it yourself: see the README. A clean build of this tag reproduces the ROM bit for bit (SHA-256 `2ee4c4babf9afb967d000034c6f77e498f23c3495aaff8eb7114cb7024e938dc`).
