**Hollowmere v1.1**: the game and the engine after a full audit of v1.0 and the repair of everything it found (59 findings).

**Download `Hollowmere-v1.1.zip`** below. It holds `Hollowmere.gba`: play it in [mGBA](https://mgba.io) or another accurate emulator, or on a GBA with a flash cartridge (it saves to SRAM). Best played in the dark, with headphones. A save from v1.0 still works: the game continues at the start of the chapter it had reached.

### The game

- **Chapter IV can be finished after dying in it.** In v1.0 a death there lost the Night Sister at prayer, and with her the ending.
- Pressing START in the frame of a death or a level exit no longer swallows it.
- **Running makes noise**, as the controls screen always said: she hears it five tiles away, three through walls.
- Furniture no longer vanishes from some angles, notes and batteries on tables can be seen from every side, and no view leaves stale pixels on screen: 20,368 views checked, none with an undrawn pixel (v1.0: 583).
- **Saves survive a power cut** (two alternating slots), and a corrupt save can no longer hang the game.
- Smoother: switching the flashlight, a subtitle appearing and the Sister re-planning her path no longer cost a frame or more.
- A dozen smaller fixes: the wardrobe view leaking into menus, a sign cut off mid-sentence, wrong colours flashing on four transitions, a rare death in the moment of winning.

### The engine

- **It stands apart from the game.** `engine/src/game_api.h` is the whole interface (nine hooks), and `examples/minimal` is a complete second game in one 180-line C file: `make GAME=examples/minimal assets all`.
- **One table of limits, enforced.** The asset tools refuse content the engine cannot handle (a 41st door, a 129th vertex, a wall standing free) in one line naming the level, event or model.
- **A test suite that can fail**: a playthrough with assertions, 38 regression tests (36 fail on v1.0), a hole sweep over every place the player can stand, 43 hostile save files, stack and frame-rate checks. `make check` runs it all.
- The asset cache cannot go stale, requirements are pinned, a clean clone builds from the README, and a GitHub workflow builds twice and compares the ROMs.
- **MIT for everything**: the game joins the engine under the MIT licence.

### Known limits

- Not every view holds 20 fps. Of 3,396 places and headings sampled, 62 fall below 19.5 fps (v1.0: 189) and 17 of those stay at 15 fps at the lowest detail level (v1.0: 9): close views of the hall staircase and the chapel's pews.
- Every timing is from the mGBA emulator. Nothing has been run on a console.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/PowerBeef/hollowmere/blob/v1.1/CHANGELOG.md).
