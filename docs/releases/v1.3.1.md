**Hollowmere v1.3.1**: one fix to v1.3.

**Download `Hollowmere-v1.3.1.zip`** below. It holds `Hollowmere.gba`: play it in [mGBA](https://mgba.io) or another accurate emulator, or on a GBA with a flash cartridge (it saves to SRAM). Saves from every earlier version work.

- **The pale line along the top of some walls is gone.** In the hall, a line of the corridor's plaster ceiling showed above the far wall, up to three rows thick, at some distances from it and not at others; the same happened, less visibly, along the foot of walls and in other rooms. The row of floor or ceiling next to a wall's edge could be sampled just beyond the wall, in the next room. It was there in every version since v1.0.

Everything else is v1.3: see its [release notes](https://github.com/PowerBeef/hollowmere/releases/tag/v1.3) and the [changelog](https://github.com/PowerBeef/hollowmere/blob/v1.3.1/CHANGELOG.md). The known limits are unchanged: a few close views of the hall staircase and the chapel's pews run at 15 fps, and nothing has been run on a console.

Building it yourself: see the README. A clean build of this tag reproduces the ROM bit for bit (SHA-256 `9bfa64ef6dd350cfc81b60586c8d150c8705d61ef2152118d4fb2dfca183e62c`).
